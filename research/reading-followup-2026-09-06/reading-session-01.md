Gigi tells stories. The narrator is describing how meeting Momo changed his storytelling.

Meaning first, if useful: Let us say his stories used to walk on foot. But suddenly, after he met Momo, they grew wings.

فَلْنَقُلْ إِنَّ حِكَايَاتِهِ كَانَتْ تَسِيرُ عَلَى الْأَقْدَامِ، وَلَكِنْ فَجْأَةً نَبَتَتْ لَهَا أَجْنِحَةٌ مُنْذُ أَنْ عَرَفَ مُومُو.

- فَلْنَقُلْ — let us say.
- تَسِيرُ عَلَى الْأَقْدَامِ — walk on foot.
- نَبَتَتْ لَهَا أَجْنِحَةٌ — wings grew for them; they grew wings. “Them” refers to the stories.
- مُنْذُ أَنْ عَرَفَ مُومُو — since he met/came to know Momo.

Read for the image. Look at help whenever a phrase holds up the meaning. If useful, reread once while imagining the stories walking and then taking flight. There is no target time and no need to memorize the sentence.
