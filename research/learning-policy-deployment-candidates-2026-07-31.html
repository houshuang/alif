The inference

Spacing and break recovery matter, but Alif already has acquisition spacing gates, intro throttling, lapsed-word recovery, and a rapid-retest credit guard. The remaining gap is representational: the canonical lemma is the right scheduling unit, but it is not sufficient evidence that every meaningful form is retrievable.

The 4.8-point novel-form penalty and 7.7-point verb penalty are large enough to test. They are not causal proof that targeted form scheduling helps, which is why treatment remains randomized.

What “serviceable” means

  • First successful exposure to a meaningful non-citation form.
  • The word is past acquisition and has no open form episode.
  • A different, reviewable sentence containing exactly that form exists.
  • The same lemma/form pair has never been assigned before.

Median alternate material: one sentence; mean: 1.73. Capacity uses the sentence pool at the cutoff, so this is a prospective deployment estimate—not a claim that every alternate existed historically.

Two endpoints, two jobs

Operational primary: successful exact-form review in a different sentence within 14 days, with no delivery counted as failure. Ordinary historical scheduling achieved 38.3%. This tests whether the intervention actually creates successful form retrieval.

Learning secondary: the first later all-word rating. Its reconstructed success rate is already 95.5%, so it has a hard ceiling: detecting a four-point improvement needs about 486 evaluable episodes, roughly 184 active days.

Milestones: inspect balance and gross failure modes at 40; descriptive first read at 80; efficacy read at 200. Two hundred assignments should take about 66 active learning days.

Alternatives that failed the deployment gate

REJECT

Ignore early collateral successes in FSRS

78.4% of eligible FSRS sentence events occurred before the card was due, and 73.2% were collateral successes, so the hypothesis looked important. But full event replay barely changed predictive quality and did not materially change end workload. A rank-preserving calibration correction is not automatically a learning intervention.

REJECT

Globally boost weak forms in sentence selection

The first replay found seven more weak forms but displaced seven other reviewable words in small sessions. That violates the all-word principle: improving one form by silently sacrificing other words is not free.

INERT

Allow weak-form swaps only under strict dominance

Requiring every due word and every reviewable content word to remain present eliminated all regressions—but also all swaps in 12 depletion-stressed requests. The current sentence pool offers too few true dominance opportunities. Safe, but not worth deploying.

How this compares with research

Findings that agree

Alif's +4.6-point adjusted effect for distributed days is directionally consistent with controlled vocabulary studies showing benefits from practice spread across sessions. Contextual-diversity experiments also tend to benefit recall and recognition when encounter count is held fixed.

Work on inflected-word processing finds surface-form frequency effects, supporting the idea that a lemma-level memory trace does not make every form equally accessible.

Findings that are weaker or unresolved

Alif's adjusted context-variety estimate was only +0.6 points with an interval crossing zero—much weaker than controlled contextual-diversity experiments. That could reflect confounding with spacing, insufficiently distinct sentences, or a crude diversity metric.

No literature comparison makes the 4.8-point Arabic novel-form penalty causal. It is a strong diagnostic and experiment trigger, not a license to ship targeted scheduling without randomization.

  • Spacing, Feedback, and Testing Boost Vocabulary Learning in a Web Application — multi-session experimental vocabulary learning.
  • Nakata (2017), within-session repeated retrieval — higher delayed scores from more retrievals, but one retrieval most efficient when time is controlled.
  • Frances et al. (2020), contextual diversity — more texts improved recall, recognition, and meaning matching with encounter count held constant.
  • Alegre & Gordon, processing English inflection — response times predicted by surface-form frequency.
  • Rogers, Révész & Rebuschat, incidental inflection learning — adults learned morphological regularities without verbalizable rules.

Deployment contract

Enable

ALIF_PROACTIVE_FORM_EXPERIMENT=1

Restart the backend, then verify assignment events with trigger_kind=successful_first_form_exposure. The flag is documented in backend/.env.example.

Rollback

ALIF_PROACTIVE_FORM_EXPERIMENT=0

Restart. No new proactive episodes will be created. Existing episodes remain analyzable, and the original yellow-confusion pilot continues unchanged.

Stop conditions: more than one treatment slot in a session; any acquisition episode; assignment balance outside 40–60% after 40 episodes; day-14 endpoint completeness below 75%; or treatment lowering next all-word success by more than five points at a milestone.
