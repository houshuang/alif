## Visit 1 — A listener changes the storyteller

Recap: Gigi's stories grew wings after he met Momo. Now we see what happens when she listens.

Useful phrases: **خَيَالَهُ يَزْدَهِرُ** — his imagination blossoms; **مُرُوجِ الرَّبِيعِ** — spring meadows; **فَيَتَزَاحَمُ حَوْلَهُ** — so they crowd around him.

وَخَاصَّةً عِنْدَمَا تَكُونُ مُومُو مَوْجُودَةً تَسْتَمِعُ إِلَيْهِ فَإِنَّ خَيَالَهُ يَزْدَهِرُ كَمَا فِي مُرُوجِ الرَّبِيعِ فَيَتَزَاحَمُ حَوْلَهُ الْأَطْفَالُ وَالْكِبَارُ.

Meaning: Especially when Momo was there listening to him, his imagination blossomed like a spring meadow, and children and grown-ups crowded around him.

## Visit 2 — Even Gigi wants to hear the ending

Recap: With Momo listening, Gigi's stories can continue for days. He surprises himself too.

Useful phrases: **إِلَى نَفْسِهِ بِشَوْقٍ** — to himself eagerly; **إِلَى أَيِّ مَدًى** — how far; **سَيَقُودُهُ خَيَالُهُ** — his imagination will lead him.

وَبِالْمُنَاسَبَةِ فَإِنَّهُ كَانَ يَسْتَمِعُ إِلَى نَفْسِهِ بِشَوْقٍ كَذَلِكَ؛ لِأَنَّهُ لَمْ يَكُنْ يَعْرِفُ إِلَى أَيِّ مَدًى سَيَقُودُهُ خَيَالُهُ.

Meaning: And he listened to himself eagerly too, because he did not know how far his imagination would lead him.

If the last phrase needs unpacking: **سَيَقُودُهُ** = will lead him; **خَيَالُهُ** = his imagination, the thing doing the leading.

## Visit 3 — A strange demand

Recap: Tourists arrive. Gigi invents a story about an angry empress and a king with a golden fish.

This portion is an **easier retelling**, not a quotation from the book. Invented names and the long original threat are deferred. If the first two portions remain tiring, use this retelling first rather than increasing difficulty.

أَرَادَتِ الْإِمْبِرَاطُورَةُ مِنَ الْمَلِكِ أَنْ يُعْطِيَهَا سَمَكَتَهُ الذَّهَبِيَّةَ. وَهَدَّدَتْ بِقَتْلِ شَعْبِهِ إِذَا رَفَضَ.

Meaning: The empress wanted the king to give her his golden fish. She threatened to kill his people if he refused.

Help: **أَنْ يُعْطِيَهَا** — to give her; **سَمَكَتَهُ الذَّهَبِيَّةَ** — his golden fish; **إِذَا رَفَضَ** — if he refused.
