## Ranked divergences

### Real gaps

1. **The sense check runs per entry in two places but per surface in a third.** Snap chips (discover.py:797-803) and pre-read (preread_service.py:552-556) send one item per lemma with up to five forms. Prematch sends one item per surface because the resolver files different words under one entry (عيون and عيّنت both → عَيْن). Its docstring (snap_prematch.py:1-20) says one verdict per entry "then mislabels the other". On the chips path, a "different" verdict replaces the whole chip (discover.py:830-838). On pre-read, it re-routes the whole learn word. **Share:** a `build_sense_items(words, text)` that makes one item per surface, plus one `apply_sense_verdicts` that folds the per-surface verdicts back into entries. Pre-read and the chips should both use it.

2. **One Snap page does its work twice.** `_finish_snap` (773) runs the chips and the prematch side by side. Both call `check_senses` on the same matched words, so the Sonnet call and the gloss-gate call happen twice, and so does the `regloss` compare-and-set attempt. Both also call `_gloss` on the same unknown words: the chips use `count=5`, the prematch `_unmatched_words` uses `count=1000` (138). A chip and a tap on the same word can therefore show two different Haiku glosses or citation forms. **Share:** run the prematch first and pick the chips (top N by `common_first`, not yet learning, not names) from its stored words and verdicts. Delete `_sense_check_chips`.

3. **Pre-read's sense work is thrown away and redone in Snap.** Pre-read stores each photo as a `SnapPage` (preread.py:100-106) and the pre-read screen opens those pages in Snap (frontend `app/preread.tsx:217`). `analyze_pages` already sense-checks the pages' words but stores no `prematch_json`, so opening a page starts a fresh prematch (discover.py:985), which shows "pending" first and costs another round of Sonnet calls. **Share:** pre-read runs `prematch_page` per page (its OCR already runs per page) and builds `learn`/`shaky` from those per-surface verdicts. That also fixes gaps 1 and 4 for pre-read.

4. **Pre-read's check cuts off long batches.** `check_senses` truncates `PAGES` to 24,000 characters (preread_service.py:59, 416), but pre-read joins 10–20 pages into one text (635-640). Forms that appear only after the cut are judged without their passage. This is an estimate: at about 1.5–2.5k characters per page, a 20-page batch passes the limit. Running the check per page (gap 3) removes the limit.

5. **The "different" verdict is handled three ways.** Snap uses `_resolve_different_word` (discover.py:603): it looks up the checked citation form, accepts an existing entry only if it passes `_candidate_matches_correction`, and otherwise proposes the new word with the verdict's gloss. Story import (story_service.py ~465-480, ~927-941) keeps the verdict's `lemma_ar`/`pos`, **drops the verdict's gloss** in favour of the batch translation, and accepts any exact bare match with no sense gate. The textbook scan (ocr_service.py:864-883) **ignores the verdict entirely**: the key is neither accepted nor skipped, so the word is created from CAMeL's base form and the context-free `_step3` gloss. **Share:** move `_resolve_different_word` into a service (for example `services/sense_resolution.py`) and call it from all three.

6. **New words get three different gloss prompts, and only one keeps the "general meaning" rule.** `_gloss` enforces the hard invariant that a stored gloss is the word's general meaning (discover.py:240-255). Story and book import send a context window and ask only for "dictionary-form" glosses. They use **`model_override="openai"`, the paid API instead of the CLI** (story_service.py ~778), with `json_mode` and no schema. That is the exact setting that produced the أَبْصَرَ "was born" bug. The textbook path uses `json_mode=True` with no `json_schema` (ocr_service.py:262), against the CLAUDE.md rule, and glosses with no context. **Share:** make `_gloss` a public service function, `gloss_new_words(items)`. Story and book pass the context window as `example`; the textbook passes none. Keep `classify_lemmas` as a separate step where it is used now.

7. **Plain matches are never sense-checked on the textbook, story and book paths.** Only CAMeL guesses and homograph ties are checked (ocr_service.py:795; story_service.py:359). The Snap measurement found 14–24% of matched surfaces were another word (snap_prematch.py docstring). The textbook writes learner state on a match: `start_acquisition(learner_pick=True)` at 125. A bare-skeleton collision (مَلِك/مَلَك) therefore enrols the wrong word. The cheap fix needs no model call: the scan already has an English gloss for every word, so run `_candidate_matches_correction` on it as `/add` does (discover.py:1151), or reuse `_create_and_introduce`'s lookup-and-gate block outright. Story and book: a StoryWord link carries review credit when the page is completed. `import_processed_book` has a full contextual pass (`_verify_processed_book_story_mappings`, book_import_service.py:806), but `import_book` and `import_story` do not.

8. **Story and book import hold the SQLite write lock across model calls.** `import_story` (story_service.py:1729-1730) and `import_book` (book_import_service.py:514-515) call `db.add(story)` and `db.flush()`, add the StoryWords, then call `_import_unknown_words`. Its Phase 2 translation calls (~758) run while the session is still dirty. The one exception is when `_check_story_guesses` ran and committed first (~423). This breaks CLAUDE.md rule 10. The docstring's claim that the function is "structured to avoid holding the DB write lock" holds only for its own writes. **Fix:** commit before Phase 2 unconditionally, or create the Story only after the model calls.

9. **The textbook pipeline has two copies, and only the dead one has the headword fix.** `process_textbook_page` uses CAMeL's vocalized `lex` as `lemma_ar`, so surfaces like الْمَاشِي are not stored as headwords (ocr_service.py:1118-1149). The live `process_batch` stores the OCR surface (`lemma_ar=arabic`, 1558). Both copies also build their lookup from every lemma, variants included (990, 1429), instead of `build_comprehensive_lemma_lookup`. **Fix:** delete `process_textbook_page`, port the headword choice into `process_batch`, and use the comprehensive lookup.

10. **Three "does it have enough sentences?" checks have drifted.** The textbook check counts only `Sentence.target_lemma_id` (ocr_service.py:1276-1283). Discover (`_ensure_material`, discover.py:1048) and pre-read (`_prepare_material`, preread.py:239) also count `SentenceWord` occurrences. Only pre-read runs `generate_paired_material`. **Share:** one `ensure_material(lemma_ids, batch=True)` in `material_generator`.

### Smaller gaps

11. **The textbook's gloss gate gets no sentence.** `gate_widenings` falls back to `_clause_with(text, form)` (preread_service.py:462), which returns a "word — translation" line. The gate approves or refuses a widening without seeing the word in use.
12. **The two sense-check prompts and validators have drifted.** `check_lookup_sense` has a `name` verdict and the clitic/root examples (أمضت → أَمْضَى, ابنها → اِبْن). `check_senses` has neither. Validation is written twice: inline in preread_service.py:311-338 and in `lookup_sense.validate_verdict` (120). `check_senses` should call `validate_verdict` per row, and the two prompts should share their verdict definitions.
13. **Discover hides CAMeL guesses from the sense check.** `discover_words_in_text` keeps `is_guess` matches (headword-only under `strict_fallback`) and does not tell the check which matches were guesses. Snap, pre-read and the tap path could tag them the way `_mapping_provenance_tag` tags them for the mapping verifier (sentence_validator.py:3482).
14. **Failure policy differs by design but is undocumented.** The chips fail open, the prematch leaves the tap to retry, pre-read and the textbook guesses fail closed, and textbook ties keep the resolver's answer. One table in `docs/` would stop these from drifting further.

### Cosmetic duplication (low value)

- Lemma rows are built inline in four places (`_create_and_introduce`, `process_batch`, `_import_unknown_words`, `practice_names`). A shared `new_import_lemma(...)` is only worth doing together with gaps 6 and 7.
- `_call_gemini_vision` logs only successful calls to `llm_calls_*.jsonl` (ocr_service.py:226-240). Each caller also parses its own result-shape variants.
- The hand-written variant ULK reset in `process_batch` (1630-1645) belongs in `canonical_resolution` or `run_quality_gates`.

## Note on `scripts/bookify_arabic.py`

Not an OCR path. `classify_token` passes the stripped `bare` form to `lookup_lemma_id(..., strict_fallback=True)` with no provenance (187). That drops the tashkeel the resolver needs and accepts CAMeL guesses. `introduce` writes `UserLemmaKnowledge` rows directly instead of using `start_acquisition` (~1280).

## Suggested order

Gap 8 first: it is a correctness rule and the fix is small. Then gaps 2 and 3, which make the per-surface prematch the single sense-check engine for every page-based surface; that also settles gaps 1 and 4. Then gaps 5 and 6, a shared service for "different" verdicts and for new-word glosses, which story, book and textbook adopt. Then gap 7 for the textbook (deterministic gate), and gaps 9 and 10.
